Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding state licensure, compact nursing privileges, joint commission compliance, and confidentiality protections.

verified

Are HDDP placement services free for clinicians?

Yes, 100% free. Healthcare organizations and staffing agency partners fund our recruitment coordination. You never pay application fees, credentialing fees, or placement charges.

public

How does the Nurse Licensure Compact (NLC) work?

If you hold an active multi-state license from an eNLC home state, you can immediately practice in 40+ compact states without additional state board licenses. Our credential team handles multi-state validation automatically.

security

How is my clinical confidentiality protected?

Your records are stored in encrypted, HIPAA-compliant repository vaults. We never submit your candidate profile to any hospital or client without first discussing the location, rate, and obtaining your approval.

medical_information

Do you help with state licensure in non-compact states?

Absolutely. For high-demand non-compact states (e.g., California, New York, Washington), our credentialing desk offers licensure filing guidance, expedited fingerprint processing coordination, and license fee reimbursements on qualified contracts.

calendar_month

What contract lengths are available?

Contracts range from 4 to 26 weeks, with 13 weeks being standard for travel nursing. We also offer per diem flexible shift matching and permanent direct-hire placement.

receipt_long

When does clinical pay and insurance commence?

Weekly direct deposits commence the Friday following your first worked week. Comprehensive medical, dental, and vision insurance options begin on day one of your placement.
