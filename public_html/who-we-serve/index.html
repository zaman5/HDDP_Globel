Partner With HDDP

An Extension of Your Recruitment Team

Work with HDDP to expand sourcing capacity, improve recruitment coordination, and support your team during periods of high demand.

Who we partner with

We primarily support established U.S. healthcare staffing agencies and other organizations whose operating model and requirements are compatible with recruitment-only and back-office support services.

Partnership models

  • Talent Sourcing Partner

    Share role requirements and candidate criteria with us. We source and screen potential candidates for your team to review and progress through your own hiring process.

  • Dedicated Recruitment Support

    Add one or more recruiters who focus on your agreed roles, pipelines, and recruitment metrics.

  • Recruitment Operations Support

    Delegate defined administrative tasks such as interview coordination, candidate follow-up, requisition updates, and pipeline reporting.

  • Documentation Workflow Support

    Improve visibility over candidate documentation through organized checklists, follow-ups, and status tracking. Formal credentialing and compliance decisions remain with the responsible organization.

What you can expect

  • A clearly defined scope of work and points of contact
  • Agreed response times and reporting cadence
  • Candidate summaries aligned with your requirements
  • Transparent communication about pipeline progress
  • A flexible model that can scale with demand

How a partnership begins

  1. Introductory discussion and needs assessment
  2. Agreement on scope, responsibilities, data handling, and commercial terms
  3. Recruitment workflow and reporting setup
  4. Pilot requisitions or a defined support period
  5. Review of results and adjustment of the engagement
