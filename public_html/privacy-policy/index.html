The short version

  • We collect what you send us — mainly job applications, CVs and business enquiries — and use it to match candidates with healthcare roles.
  • We share candidate profiles only with partner agencies and employers considering you for a role. We verify licences with nursing boards but do not run background checks ourselves.
  • We only text you if you opt in, and we never share your mobile number for marketing.
  • We do not sell your personal information or use it for targeted advertising.
  • The site uses only one essential cookie unless you choose otherwise. See our cookie policy.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time: staffing@hddpconsultants.com.

Who we are

HDDP Consultants ([LEGAL ENTITY NAME], registered address [REGISTERED ADDRESS]) is a healthcare recruitment partner. Licence verification and background checks

Patient-safety rules and accreditation standards for healthcare staffing (such as The Joint Commission’s Health Care Staffing Services certification) require clinical staff to be checked against primary sources. As part of screening we may:

  • verify your nursing or professional licence, its status and any public disciplinary action with the issuing state board of nursing or Nursys®, including multistate (NLC) privileges;
  • check the US HHS-OIG List of Excluded Individuals/Entities (LEIE) and the SAM.gov exclusion list, which are public records;
  • contact referees you have named, with your permission.

We do not order background checks, criminal record checks, credit reports, drug tests or other “consumer reports”. If a partner agency or employer does, the US Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA) and state laws require it to give you a separate written disclosure, get your written authorization first and, before any adverse decision based on the report, give you a copy of the report and a summary of your rights. Many states and cities also limit when employers may ask about criminal history (“fair chance” or “ban-the-box” laws). We do not ask about it.

The employer verifies work authorization after a job offer, using Form I-9 and, where it is used, E-Verify. We never ask for identity or immigration documents before then.

Who we share it with

  • Partner agencies and healthcare employers considering you for a role. They decide whether to hire you and handle your data under their own privacy notices.
  • Service providers that host our website, email and data, under written contracts that let them use it only on our instructions and keep it secure.
  • Professional advisers such as lawyers and accountants, under a duty of confidentiality.
  • Authorities when the law requires it, or to protect rights, safety and property.
  • A buyer or successor if our business is reorganised or sold, under the same protections.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, as those terms are defined in US state privacy laws. We have not done so in the last 12 months.

Calls, texts and emails

We contact candidates by email and phone about the applications they send us and about similar roles.

  • Text messages are sent only if you tick the separate, optional text-message box on our forms. Agreeing is not a condition of applying. We send up to 4 messages a week; message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help.
  • We do not share your mobile number or text-message opt-in with third parties or affiliates for their marketing. Text-message opt-in data and consent are excluded from all sharing described in this policy.
  • Emails about new roles include an unsubscribe link, and we act on it promptly, as the US CAN-SPAM Act, UK PECR and Canada’s Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL) require.
  • We do not use autodialed or pre-recorded calls without your consent, and we respect the US National Do Not Call Registry for marketing calls.

Security

CVs are not publicly accessible and only authorised staff can open them. We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls and staff confidentiality obligations, and we only give access to people who need it. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach is likely to affect you, we will tell you and the relevant regulator as the law requires — in the UK within 72 hours of becoming aware of a reportable breach, and in the US as your state’s breach-notification law requires.

How to use your rights

Email staffing@hddpconsultants.com or use our contact form, and tell us which country you live in. To protect you, we will check your identity before sharing or changing data — usually by matching the email address and details you gave us. You may ask someone to act for you as your authorised agent; we will ask for proof that they are allowed to.

We reply within the time the law sets: one month in the UK, 30 days in Canada and 45 days in the US. These can be extended where the law allows, and we will tell you if they are. Requests are free unless they are clearly unfounded or excessive. We will not treat you differently for using your rights.

Children

This website and our services are for adults seeking work and for businesses. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Recruitment scams

We never ask candidates to pay a fee, buy equipment or share bank or card details to apply or to be placed. Our emails come only from @hddpconsultants.com addresses. If someone contacts you claiming to be from HDDP Consultants and asks for money, do not respond — forward the message to staffing@hddpconsultants.com.

Equal opportunity and accessibility

We consider every qualified candidate without regard to race, colour, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, age, disability, genetic information, veteran or military status, pregnancy, marital status, citizenship status (where protected) or any other characteristic protected by law in the United States, the United Kingdom (Equality Act 2010) or Canada (Canadian Human Rights Act and provincial human rights codes).

In the United States this includes Title VII of the Civil Rights Act, the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA), the Age Discrimination in Employment Act, the Genetic Information Nondiscrimination Act (GINA), the Pregnant Workers Fairness Act and the anti-discrimination provisions of the Immigration and Nationality Act, as well as state and local laws. In line with state and local salary-history bans, we never ask about your current or past pay, and our job listings show a pay range.

If you need an accommodation or reasonable adjustment to apply — for example a different format or help completing the form — email staffing@hddpconsultants.com or call (800) 555-4337. We aim for this website to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 level AA; if any part of it is hard to use, please tell us.

Licensing and right-to-work requirements — for example an active RN or LPN/LVN licence in the state where you will work (or a multistate NLC licence), NCLEX, and BLS/ACLS or specialty certifications in the US; NMC registration in the UK; or provincial college registration in Canada — are set by the partner agency or employer for each role.
